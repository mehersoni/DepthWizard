## 8. Generalization Analysis & Key Findings
1. **Full Dataset Generalization**: Refined M4 generalizes robustly across the entire Potsdam dataset of 1760 buildings, achieving **`4.69 m` MAE** and **`3.32 m` MedAE** (compared to `13.50 m` MAE for baseline).
2. **Tile-to-Tile Consistency**: Performance is remarkably consistent across all 37 active tiles, with median errors consistently between 2.0m and 5.5m.
3. **Building Size Behavior**: Medium buildings ($4-12\text{m}$) perform best, followed by Small buildings. Large buildings ($>12\text{m}$) have slightly higher error primarily due to complex roof structure shadows.
4. **Dominant Failure Mode**: `FALSE_LONG_SHADOW` (over-extension into dark vegetation/asphalt) represents 13.8% of all buildings.
